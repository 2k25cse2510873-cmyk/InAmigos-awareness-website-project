About InAmigos Foundation

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization founded on 23 September 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla, Founder & CEO.

The Foundation works towards community development and social empowerment through initiatives focused on education, food distribution, women empowerment, animal welfare, environmental sustainability and skill development.

InAmigos Foundation believes in the power of collective action and works with volunteers, professionals and partners to create positive social change.

Section 8 Registered NGO
80G & 12A Certified
CSR-1 Registered
ISO 9001:2015 Certified
